Our Services

Care for every
stage of life

We offer a full spectrum of healthcare services under one roof — from annual checkups to specialty care, all delivered with compassion.

Primary Care

Our primary care team provides comprehensive healthcare for patients of all ages. We focus on preventive medicine, early detection, and management of chronic conditions to keep you at your best.

Building a long-term relationship with a primary care provider is one of the most important investments in your health. We'll be there through every season of life.

  • Annual wellness exams & physical checkups
  • Chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension, asthma)
  • Preventive screenings and immunizations
  • Acute illness visits (cold, flu, infections)
  • Health risk assessments
  • Referrals to specialists
Book a Primary Care Visit

Pediatrics

From newborn care to adolescent medicine, our pediatric team provides compassionate, evidence-based care for children of all ages. We partner with parents to support healthy development.

  • Newborn and infant care
  • Well-child visits and developmental screenings
  • Childhood vaccinations per CDC schedule
  • School and sports physicals
  • Adolescent medicine and ADHD management
  • Sick visits for common childhood illnesses
Schedule a Pediatric Visit

Mental Health

Mental health is just as important as physical health. Our integrated behavioral health services provide support for a range of conditions with a judgment-free, compassionate approach.

  • Depression and anxiety evaluation & management
  • Medication management and consultation
  • Stress and burnout counseling
  • Behavioral health screenings
  • Referrals to licensed therapists and psychiatrists
  • Telehealth mental health visits
Book a Mental Health Appointment

Women's Health

Our women's health services provide comprehensive, compassionate care through every phase of a woman's life — from adolescence through menopause and beyond.

  • Annual GYN exams and Pap smears
  • Prenatal and postpartum care
  • Contraception counseling and management
  • Menopause management
  • Breast health screenings and referrals
  • STI testing and treatment
Book a Women's Health Visit

Lab & Diagnostics

Our on-site laboratory allows for fast, accurate testing so your provider can make informed decisions the same day. We also coordinate imaging and specialty referrals as needed.

  • Comprehensive blood panels (CBC, metabolic, lipid)
  • Urinalysis and culture
  • Rapid strep, flu, and COVID testing
  • Thyroid and hormone panels
  • EKG (electrocardiogram)
  • Imaging referrals (X-ray, MRI, ultrasound)
Schedule Lab Work

Telehealth

See your provider from the comfort of home via secure video visit. Telehealth is available 7 days a week for many conditions — no travel, no waiting room.

  • Follow-up visits and prescription refills
  • Minor illness evaluation (UTI, cold, rash)
  • Mental health support sessions
  • Lab result reviews
  • Chronic disease check-ins
  • Available via smartphone, tablet, or computer
Start a Telehealth Visit
